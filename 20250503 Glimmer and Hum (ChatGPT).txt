Here’s a simple and elegant serving suggestion to pair fresh figs with minced fish bean curd rolls:

🌸 Fusion Appetizer Plate: Figs & Bean Curd Rolls

Ingredients:
	•	Fresh ripe figs (green or purple)
	•	Minced fish bean curd rolls (steamed or pan-fried)
	•	Optional: microgreens or shiso leaves for garnish
	•	Optional: dipping sauce (light soy sauce with a dash of sesame oil and rice vinegar)

Presentation:
	1.	Cut the figs in quarters or halves depending on size. If small, whole is fine.
	2.	Place 2–3 bean curd rolls on one side of a plate or shallow dish.
	3.	Arrange the fig pieces on the opposite side, fan-like, or scattered artfully.
	4.	Add a small ramekin of the dipping sauce in the center or off to the side.
	5.	Garnish with microgreens, edible flowers, or thinly sliced scallions for color.

Serving Tip:

Offer it slightly warm—the contrast between warm bean curd and cool fresh figs is delightful. The fig’s honey-like sweetness balances the savory roll, especially with a touch of dipping sauce.
